HOW TO PLAY

2 Players On 1 Device Player 1 stays on the left, Player 2 on the right. Players take turns answering grammar questions.
Attacks & Combos Clicking the correct comparative or superlative answer hits your opponent! Consecutive correct answers stack big combo streaks!
Misses & Timeouts Wrong answers or running out of the 10-second timer result in a miss. Turn immediately passes to your opponent.
Ultimate Attacks Fill your Ultimate Meter to 100%. Your next correct hit triggers a high-damage finisher move!
